# Small Language Models That Run on a Laptop

## Summary
Small Language Models (SLMs) are a subset of artificial intelligence models designed to be more accessible and efficient for users with limited computational resources. These models are typically smaller and faster than large language models (LLMs) and can be run on standard laptops without the need for cloud connectivity. The rise of SLMs is driven by the need to make AI more accessible for various applications, from chatbots to specialized tasks, while addressing the limitations of LLMs in terms of computational power and resource requirements.

## Findings
### Model Architecture and Performance
- SLMs, like LLMs, use the transformer model architecture, which has become fundamental in natural language processing (NLP) [2].
- There is a trend towards more computationally light alternatives, such as Root Mean Square Normalization (RMSNorm), which is featured in many recent SLM architectures [4].

### Practical Applications and Tools
- SLMs are designed to work on standard laptops and deliver production-grade results for specialized tasks [9].
- Several tools and models are available for running SLMs locally, including LM Studio, Unsloth, Ollama, and LocalAI [6, 7].
- The best local LLM tools in 2026 are free, run entirely offline, and use llama.cpp underneath, ensuring consistent model behavior [7].

### Challenges and Future Directions
- SLMs face challenges related to performance, expertise, and security [10].
- A survey on on-device SLMs highlights the need for further research in architectures, compression, and applications [11].
